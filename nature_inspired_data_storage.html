Historical Evolution

Timeline of DNA Storage Research & Breakthroughs

2012
YEAR 2012

First Next-Gen DNA Data Encoding

Lead Researcher: Prof. George Church (Harvard University)

Encoded a 53,000-word book, 11 JPG images, and a JavaScript program (700 KB total data) into DNA microchips, proving synthetic DNA as a viable storage medium.

2017
YEAR 2017

DNA Fountain & 215 PB/g Benchmark

Columbia University & New York Genome Center

Published the "DNA Fountain" mathematical strategy in *Science*, reaching theoretical density of 215 Petabytes per gram with robust error correction for flawless retrieval.

2019
YEAR 2019

Fully Automated DNA Data Storage End-to-End

Microsoft Research & University of Washington

Demonstrated the world's first fully automated system to encode, synthesize, preserve, and decode digital data (storing and playing back HD media files including the film *Arrival*).

2024
YEAR 2024

Epigenetic Printing & Multi-Bit DNA Writing

Nature Scientific Reports

Introduction of epigenetic methyl-mark modification on natural DNA backbones, bypassing expensive step-by-step de novo synthesis and lowering write costs.

2025
YEAR 2025

CRISPR-Cas9 Precise Random Access Breakthrough

Advanced Molecular Computing Labs

Development of targeted CRISPR RNA-guided search mechanisms allowing instant random-access querying of specific digital files within massive multi-exabyte DNA pools without sequencing the entire pool.
